## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the intersection of high-performance athletics and restorative sanctuary. Designed for endurance athletes, active professionals, and wellness enthusiasts, it conveys clinical precision, grounded vitality, and mindful restoration. The aesthetic avoids clinical coldness and chaotic fitness energy; instead, it establishes an elevated, calm, and architectural environment reminiscent of high-end Nordic wellness spaces.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Modern Editorial Wellness meets Quiet Luxury.
- **Atmosphere:** Natural grounding through rich pine, damp slate, warm sand, and fired clay. It balances technical athletic tracking (recovery metrics, scheduled sessions) with tactile warmth (wet sauna steam, kinesthetic touch).
- **Core Visual Drivers:** High-contrast editorial typography paired with razor-sharp geometric UI primitives, whisper-soft hairline borders (`1px` solid with dialed alpha channels), dual-toned split panels, and restrained atmospheric radial glows.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the natural sensory markers of physical rehabilitation: evergreen forests, natural clay, hot stone, and mist.

### Role Mapping
- **Primary (`#1E2E25` - Forest Deep):** The foundational core. Serves as high-contrast primary call-to-action buttons, key brand badges, and dominant split-screen hero panels. Evokes grounded stability and strength.
- **Secondary (`#D27C56` - Terracotta Clay):** The kinetic recovery accent. Employed for progress indicators, interactive badges, accent highlights, focal callouts, and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#EBDCCB` - Warm Sandstone):** The restorative neutral. Used for sub-surface backdrops, secondary segmented tabs, badge containers, and soft contrast panels that soften stark white canvas areas.
- **Neutral (`#14221A` - Deep Pine Onyx):** Primary typography anchor and absolute dark field. Paired against off-white grounds (`#FBFBF9`) for readability without harsh optical vibration.

### Surface Tones & Semantic States
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBFBF9` (Clean, warm alabaster).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Neutral):** `#FFFFFF` with `#1E2E25` borders at `8%` opacity.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Muted Card):** `#F7F4EE` for secondary actions and service directory cards.
- **Success / Recovery Confirmation:** `#3A6351` (Organic Herb Green).
- **Text Primary:** `#14221A` on light surfaces; `#FFFFFF` / `#F3EFEA` on dark forest surfaces.
- **Text Muted:** `#6A7B72` on light surfaces; `#9FB3A8` on deep forest surfaces.

## Typography

The type system blends modern editorial prestige with athletic precision.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Editorial Voice (Newsreader):** Used for primary titles, hero mantras, section headings, and quotes. When used for statement lines, the italic variant (e.g., *"Vuelve más fuerte"*) introduces human touch, craftsmanship, and kinesthetic motion.
- **Functional Interface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Applied to dashboards, data readouts, metric badges, form labels, and interactive navigational items. Its clean geometry maintains high legibility across mobile screens and desktop reservation tables.
- **Micro-Copy & Metadata (`caption-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.14em`) for category pills, session timestamps, and kicker lines above headlines (e.g., `AGENDA · RECUPERACIÓN · PERFORMANCE`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances asymmetrical editorial storytelling on large displays with a unified single-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Grid & Composition Rules
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** Uses an asymmetrical two-column layout. The left column (typically 45–50% width) hosts dark forest atmospheric branding, photography, and contextual metrics. The right column sits on an alabaster ground (`#FBFBF9`) dedicated to modular task flows (onboarding, service bookings, authenticated account controls).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Collapses into a centered 8-column layout with 2rem margins and 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Switches to a 4-column layout with 1.25rem side margins. The deep forest panel condenses into a compact hero header with branding and essential status chips, while the task flow occupies the main body stack.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Strict 4px/8px incremental grid. Interactive tap targets conform to a minimum height of 48px to accommodate post-workout finger dexterity.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids heavy, artificial drop shadows in favor of architectural layering and subtle light diffusion.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Completely flat `#FBFBF9` or `#14221A`.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Inputs):** Supported by a crisp hairline border (`1px solid rgba(30, 46, 37, 0.12)`) and an ambient, low-opacity spread: `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px -2px rgba(20, 34, 26, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Action Cards & Modals):** Used for focal schedule units and floating CTA panels: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(20, 34, 26, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(20, 34, 26, 0.03)`.
- **Atmospheric Glow (Dark Mode / Forest Canvas):** A radial gradient backdrop layer (`radial-gradient(circle at top right, rgba(210, 124, 86, 0.12), transparent 70%)`) that mimics ambient sauna warmth through steam.
- **Glass & Frosted Elements:** Top banners and session state pills use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over deep green surfaces with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` fill and `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` borders.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, ergonomic geometry (`Level 2: Rounded`). Elements feel approachable and organic, matching human anatomy and massage stones, without dissolving into overly playful toy-like rounds.

### Application Tokens
- **Standard Corners (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`):** Checkboxes, form fields, mini badges, date blocks.
- **Large Containers (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Service directory action cards, account dialog cards, summary modules.
- **Hero Containers (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`):** Primary floating booking cards, split-pane envelopes, sauna feature modules.
- **Full Radius (`9999px` / Pill):** Filter chips, category status capsules, progress track ends, and icon button bases.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E2E25` (Deep Pine), text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem` (`8px`), font weight `600`. Hover brings a transition to `#14221A` with a subtle translate offset of `-1px`. Includes an optional inline terminal arrow (`→`).
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Background `transparent`, `1px` border in `#1E2E25` at `20%` alpha, text `#14221A`. Hover transitions fill to `rgba(30, 46, 37, 0.04)`.
- **Accent:** Background `#D27C56` (Terracotta), text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved exclusively for purchase completion, instant booking triggers, and recovery milestones.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(30, 46, 37, 0.18)`, corner radius `0.5rem`. Left-aligned line icons (featherweight stroke) in `#6A7B72`. Focus state introduces a `1px` border in `#1E2E25` and a delicate `3px` ring in `rgba(30, 46, 37, 0.10)`.
- **Checkboxes:** 20px square with a `4px` radius. Checked state displays `#1E2E25` with a white checkmark icon.

### Segmented Controls (Tab Switcher)
- Capsule enclosure with background `#EBDCCB` (Warm Sandstone) at 60% opacity, `6px` padding. Active tab features a pure white pill (`#FFFFFF`) with a featherweight elevation shadow (`0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`).

### Service Directory Cards
- Interactive navigation rows featuring light ivory/sand backplates (`#FDFBF7`), subtle hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(210, 124, 86, 0.15)`), dynamic left-hand iconography inside clay-tinted soft squares, bold title with secondary descriptive caption, and a terminal forward arrow indicator (`→`) that shifts right on hover.

### Progress & Session Tracker
- **Progress Track:** 6px thickness, track background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` on dark, active fill with gradient from `#D27C56` to `#EBDCCB`.
- **Date Stamp Callout:** Clean 48x48px calendar stone block with month uppercase kicker and bold numeric day value.

### Floating Action Badges
- Fixed bottom-right utility dials (e.g., WhatsApp direct consultation) encapsulated in high-contrast circles with a 12px shadow offset, maintaining accessibility and direct customer communication.
